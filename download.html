After you drag PowerTokensVideoStudio.app into Applications, the first time you open it the system will say the developer cannot be verified. Use either method:

  1. In Finder, right-click the app, choose Open, then confirm Open.
  2. Open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
